WHO WE ARE

A Place To Encounter God

Seeta Revival Church is a community of believers passionate about worship, prayer, the Word of God and transformed lives.

We believe that every person can experience the love, grace and transforming power of Jesus Christ.

Bringing Revival to all through the Gospel by Preaching, Teaching and Actionalising the Word till the End.

Our Mission

Spiritual Revival to bring a Physical Revival, attained through Prayer, Fasting & Reading of Scriptures to cause Change.

Our Slogan

Begin with the Spirit at the Prayer Tent.
